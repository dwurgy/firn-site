About Firn

A calm, minimalist browser with vertical tabs, spaces, and split view. Available now for Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

  • Spaces: Keep Work and Personal apart, each with its own tabs, sign-ins, and color. Switching feels like opening a different browser.
  • Basecamp: Your everyday sites, like mail and calendar, one click away at the top of the sidebar. Each space has its own.
  • Groups: Keep tabs together. Drag one tab onto another to make a group.
  • Lookout: Peek at a link in a floating panel without leaving your page. Keep it as a tab if you want it.
  • Split view: Two pages side by side.
  • Tidied away: Tabs you haven't looked at for a while move quietly into a small button, so the sidebar stays clear.
  • Nothing collected: No tracking, no telemetry, and no account. Ads and trackers are blocked by default.
